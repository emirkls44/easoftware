Privacy Policy for Pathmory

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Pathmory is developed by E&A Software. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Pathmory mobile application.

1. Information You Provide

Pathmory may allow you to enter information such as your name and email address when creating a local profile inside the application.

This profile information is stored locally on your device. Pathmory does not currently operate a server-based user account system for storing this information.

2. Photos and Media

Pathmory allows users to select or create photos and use them inside the application.

Photos selected by the user are processed and stored locally on the user's device. We do not operate a backend server that receives or stores users' personal photos.

On supported Android versions, Pathmory uses Android system features such as the system photo picker to allow users to select photos.

3. Device Storage

Pathmory may save application data and user-created content on the user's device.

Some older Android versions may require storage-related permissions in order to save content to the device.

4. Location Data

Pathmory does not currently collect, store, or track the user's precise or approximate location.

5. Internet and Server Storage

The current version of Pathmory is designed primarily around local, on-device storage.

We do not currently operate a Pathmory backend server that receives users' profiles, personal photos, or locally created memories.

Certain operating system services or third-party platform services, such as Google Play services, may use internet connectivity independently when required for their functionality.

6. Purchases and Premium Features

Pathmory may include technical support for Google Play Billing and may offer optional paid or premium features in the future.

If purchases are offered, payments will be processed by Google Play. E&A Software does not directly receive or store users' full credit card or banking information.

Google Play may process purchase-related information according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

7. Advertising

Pathmory does not currently display third-party advertisements.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or together with the relevant change.

8. Data Sharing

E&A Software does not sell users' personal information.

Because the current version stores user-created profile information and personal media locally on the device, this information is not intentionally transmitted by us to advertising companies or other third parties.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally by Pathmory remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user, removed through the application, or removed when application data is cleared or the application is uninstalled, depending on the type of data and Android system behavior.

10. Children's Privacy

Pathmory does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a server operated by E&A Software.

Parents or guardians who believe there is a privacy concern may contact us using the email address below.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps when designing Pathmory to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of personal information.

However, no device or software environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Future Changes

Pathmory may gain additional functionality in future versions, including features such as premium services, advertising, online services, or other integrations.

If these changes affect how personal information is collected, processed, stored, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with an updated "Last updated" date.
